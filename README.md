## Calculation convention

The scenario gives 34 calendar days of elapsed time and 770 minutes of working time. The primary flow-efficiency calculation therefore uses calendar time: `770 / (34 × 24 × 60) = 1.57%`. For readers who use an eight-hour business-day convention, the equivalent is `770 / (34 × 8 × 60) = 4.72%`. The conclusion is unchanged: almost all elapsed time is waiting.
